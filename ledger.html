Every request, and exactly what became of it.

This page is the channel's promise kept. The channel's selection rules that decide what gets made are fixed in advance and applied by software rather than by hand, and every eligible request ends made, queued or declined with a stated reason. Nothing is dropped silently, and nothing here is written by a person after the fact: it is generated from the channel's own records and republished automatically.

The channel is Made to Order on YouTube, where requests are made by comment. This page is rebuilt every day, so what it says is what happened — including the requests that were refused, and the entries in this ledger that were never viewer requests at all. Those are labelled rather than deleted.

Made to Order is a running experiment in community consensus mechanics. A request queue is opened to the public. Rules decide what gets made. Most episodes answer a request. When none is waiting, the channel explains one thing it found itself and names the source on screen. Every request is accounted for. The result is measured and published.
